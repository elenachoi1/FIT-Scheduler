FIT Scheduler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What FIT Scheduler is

FIT Scheduler is a free, student-built web app that helps groups find a meeting time. It is not an official app of the Fashion Institute of Technology.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Information you add: the groups you create or join, the events you create, and the time slots you mark as available.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, to show your name and availability to the members of your groups, and to suggest meeting times. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your groups.

Who can see your information

Only members of a group can see that group's events and the names and availability of its members. Security rules in the database enforce this.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your information

You can leave a group or delete groups and events you own at any time in the app. To delete your account and all of its data, contact us using the link below.
